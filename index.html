!!!!! SECTION I - HISTORY !!!!!

DISCOVER THE PAST

History

They wore crowns and carried sceptres. They also carried armies. Approach these files with respect - their kind is not gone, only sleeping.

FILE NO. 001 // ALEXANDER THE GREAT
CLASSIFICATION: King of Macedonia
THREAT LEVEL: ☠☠☠☠☠
STATUS: DECEASED - 323 BCE   LOCATION OF REMAINS: Macedonia (disputed)
FILE: King of Macedonia who conquered one of the largest empires in history, stretching from Greece to India. His military genius was legendary - and so was his ambition. When he wept, it was because there were no more worlds to take.
FILE NO. 002 // CLEOPATRA VII
CLASSIFICATION: Last Pharaoh of Ancient Egypt
THREAT LEVEL: ☠☠☠☠
STATUS: DECEASED - 30 BCE   LOCATION OF REMAINS: Unknown (the tomb has never been found)
FILE: The last pharaoh of Ancient Egypt, and a mind sharper than any blade. She charmed Julius Caesar and Mark Antony, and bent Mediterranean politics to her will. Her end is disputed. The asp knows the truth.
FILE NO. 003 // CHARLEMAGNE
CLASSIFICATION: King of the Franks
THREAT LEVEL: ☠☠☠☠
STATUS: DECEASED - 814 CE   LOCATION OF REMAINS: Aachen Cathedral
FILE: King of the Franks who united most of Western Europe during the Middle Ages. Crowned Emperor of the Romans in 800 CE. They say he founded an empire. They do not mention what he did to those who refused.
FILE NO. 004 // ASHOKA THE GREAT
CLASSIFICATION: Emperor of the Maurya Dynasty
THREAT LEVEL: ☠☠ (formerly ☠☠☠☠☠)
STATUS: DECEASED - 232 BCE   LOCATION OF REMAINS: Unknown
FILE: Emperor of the Maurya Dynasty who witnessed the horrors of war and - remarkably - chose to change. He embraced Buddhism and spread peace, non-violence, and tolerance across his empire. One of the few files marked "REDEEMED".
!!!!! SECTION II - SCIENCE !!!!!

PIONEERS OF KNOWLEDGE

Science

They pulled back the curtain on the universe and stared into things that were not meant to be seen. Their discoveries changed everything. Whether that was a good thing remains an open question.

FILE NO. 005 // ISAAC NEWTON
CLASSIFICATION: English Mathematician & Physicist
THREAT LEVEL: ☠☠☠
STATUS: DECEASED - 1727   LOCATION OF REMAINS: Westminster Abbey
FILE: English mathematician and physicist who formulated the laws of motion and universal gravitation. He wrote more about alchemy than physics. His papers, when finally opened, caused men to whisper.
FILE NO. 006 // MARIE CURIE
CLASSIFICATION: Physicist & Chemist
THREAT LEVEL: ☠☠☠☠
STATUS: DECEASED - 1934   LOCATION OF REMAINS: The Pantheon (her notebook is still radioactive)
FILE: Pioneer of radioactivity. First woman to win a Nobel Prize, and the only person to win Nobel Prizes in two different sciences. She carried glowing vials in her pockets. The glow followed her to the grave.
FILE NO. 007 // ALBERT EINSTEIN
CLASSIFICATION: Theoretical Physicist
THREAT LEVEL: ☠☠☠☠
STATUS: DECEASED - 1955   LOCATION OF REMAINS: His brain is preserved. It was examined. It was different.
FILE: Developed the theory of relativity - one of the two pillars of modern physics. His equation E=mc² revealed that mass and energy are one. The equation was elegant. What it made possible was not.
FILE NO. 008 // CHARLES DARWIN
CLASSIFICATION: English Naturalist
THREAT LEVEL: ☠☠☠☠
STATUS: DECEASED - 1882   LOCATION OF REMAINS: Westminster Abbey
FILE: English naturalist who established the theory of evolution by natural selection. He showed that all life is one long chain stretching back into the dark. Many were not ready for the answer he found in the bones.
!!!!! SECTION III - PHILOSOPHY !!!!!

SEEKERS OF WISDOM

Philosophy

They asked the questions no one else dared: What is real? What is good? What is the point? Their words outlived them. Words, it turns out, are the most dangerous things of all.

FILE NO. 009 // SOCRATES
CLASSIFICATION: Founder of Western Philosophy
THREAT LEVEL: ☠☠☠
STATUS: DECEASED - 399 BCE   LOCATION OF REMAINS: Athens (method of death: poison hemlock, by court order)
FILE: Founder of Western philosophy. His method was to ask questions until people realised they knew nothing. The city decided this was too dangerous and ordered him to drink hemlock. He asked if they were sure. They were.
FILE NO. 010 // CONFUCIUS
CLASSIFICATION: Chinese Philosopher
THREAT LEVEL: ☠☠☠
STATUS: DECEASED - 479 BCE   LOCATION OF REMAINS: Kong Forest, Qufu
FILE: Chinese philosopher whose teachings on morality, social relationships, justice, and sincerity became Confucianism. His words shaped East Asian culture for millennia. He did not start a war. His ideas simply won without one.
FILE NO. 011 // ARISTOTLE
CLASSIFICATION: Greek Philosopher & Polymath
THREAT LEVEL: ☠☠☠
STATUS: DECEASED - 322 BCE   LOCATION OF REMAINS: Chalcis, Greece
FILE: Greek polymath who wrote on physics, biology, logic, ethics, politics, and everything else he could find. He tutored Alexander the Great - the most dangerous man alive. They made a dangerous pair.
FILE NO. 012 // IMMANUEL KANT
CLASSIFICATION: German Philosopher
THREAT LEVEL: ☠☠☠
STATUS: DECEASED - 1804   LOCATION OF REMAINS: Königsberg (he never once left the city in his life)
FILE: German philosopher who argued that reason is the source of morality. His "Critique of Pure Reason" sought to unite reason with experience. The city knew him as the man who took a walk at exactly 5:00 PM, every day, for fifty years.
!!!!! SECTION IV - CIVILIZATIONS !!!!!

ANCIENT WORLDS

Civilizations

Empires that built wonders, wrote laws, and reached for the sky - and then were swallowed by time. Walk through their ruins carefully. The stones remember.

FILE NO. 013 // ANCIENT EGYPT
CLASSIFICATION: Civilization of the Nile
THREAT LEVEL: ☠☠☠☠☠
STATUS: EXTINCT - c. 30 BCE   REMAINS: The Pyramids of Giza
FILE: One of the world's oldest civilizations, known for its pyramids, pharaohs, hieroglyphics, and advances in architecture, mathematics, and medicine along the Nile. The tombs were sealed with curses. The curses, apparently, were real.
FILE NO. 014 // MESOPOTAMIA
CLASSIFICATION: Cradle of Civilization
THREAT LEVEL: ☠☠☠☠
STATUS: EXTINCT - c. 539 BCE   REMAINS: The Ziggurats of Ur
FILE: The "Cradle of Civilization" between the Tigris and Euphrates rivers. The Sumerians invented writing (cuneiform), the wheel, and irrigation, and built the first cities. They wrote the oldest stories. Some of those stories should not have been written down.
FILE NO. 015 // ANCIENT GREECE
CLASSIFICATION: Birthplace of Democracy
THREAT LEVEL: ☠☠☠☠
STATUS: EXTINCT - c. 146 BCE   REMAINS: The Parthenon
FILE: Birthplace of Western philosophy, democracy, theater, and the Olympic Games. Their art and architecture were so good that every civilization since has been copying them. Their gods were jealous, petty, and very good at curses.
FILE NO. 016 // ROMAN EMPIRE
CLASSIFICATION: The Roman Empire
THREAT LEVEL: ☠☠☠☠☠
STATUS: EXTINCT - 476 CE   REMAINS: The Colosseum (and 50,000 miles of roads)
FILE: One of history's greatest empires, famed for aqueducts, roads, and the Colosseum. Roman law and Latin shaped the Western world. They built roads that still exist. Their empire fell, but their roads - and their ideas - refused to die.
FILE NO. 017 // INDUS VALLEY
CLASSIFICATION: Bronze Age Civilization
THREAT LEVEL: ☠☠☠
STATUS: EXTINCT - c. 1300 BCE   REMAINS: Mohenjo-daro & Harappa
FILE: An advanced Bronze Age civilization with sophisticated city planning, advanced drainage, and standardized weights and measures. They built cities so well planned that we are still figuring them out. Then they vanished - and left no translation of their writing behind.
FILE NO. 018 // MAYA CIVILIZATION
CLASSIFICATION: Mesoamerican Civilization
THREAT LEVEL: ☠☠☠☠☠
STATUS: EXTINCT - c. 1500 CE   REMAINS: Chichen Itza
FILE: Mesoamerican civilization known for its hieroglyphic script, art, advanced mathematics, and precision calendars - accurate to the day, thousands of years into the future. They predicted the end of their world. It came. They were right.
